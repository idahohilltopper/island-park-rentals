arriving through Bozeman, Big Sky, West Yellowstone and Idaho Falls. Rankings are won query by query; broad terms ("Yellowstone hotels")
are dominated by Tripadvisor, VRBO, Expedia and NPS, so we win the specific searches first.

## Roadmap
1. DONE: core pages, structured data, speed/accessibility 100, IndexNow, Search Console, AI-search tracking (monthly).
2. Next pages (verify every fact first): Island Park vs Big Sky vs West Yellowstone; winter in Island Park; Grand Teton + Yellowstone itinerary;
   one page per new cabin (see ADDING-A-CABIN.md); seasonal "when to visit" guides.
3. Links and mentions (the biggest lever for a new site): Island Park Chamber of Commerce, islandparkidaho.com, yellowstoneteton.com,
   westyellowstonenet.com, regional "best cabins near Yellowstone" roundups. AI answers cite these sites heavily.
4. Reviews: keep the Airbnb listing strong. AI tools and Google cite Airbnb listings directly.
5. Measure: Search Console (linked to OpenSEO), monthly AI-visibility check, rank tracking of the saved keywords.

## Rules we follow
- Only publish facts we can source (NPS, Forest Service, state agencies, the Airbnb listing). Say "approximate" where sources differ.
- Real photos for places and cabins. No invented images of real locations.
- One topic per page, unique title under 60 characters and description under 160.
