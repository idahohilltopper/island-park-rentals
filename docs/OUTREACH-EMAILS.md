How to use: pick a section, find the contact on the target's own site (contact page, "write for us", or the email shown there),
paste the email, change the [brackets], send from your own address. Send 3 to 5 a week, not all at once. Track replies at the bottom.

## A. Free listings you apply for (no pitch needed, highest value)

| Where | What to ask for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Island Park Area Chamber of Commerce (islandparkchamber.org) | Membership and a lodging listing linking to yellowstonegatewaystays.com | Most cited local authority. Ask what the listing page allows (link, photos, description). |
| Yellowstone Teton Territory (yellowstoneteton.com) | A lodging listing | Regional tourism board. Ask about listing requirements and fees before agreeing to anything. |
| Visit Idaho (visitidaho.org) | Ask how lodging and itinerary content is submitted | May be limited to members or partners. |
| Google Business Profile | Only if you can qualify (it needs a legitimate business presentation and verification) | Do not publish a cabin address. Follow Google's rules for rentals. |
| Airbnb listing | Add yellowstonegatewaystays.com in the host bio if Airbnb allows it | Check Airbnb's current rules on external links. |

## E. Local newsletters and community pages

Ask local Facebook groups and community pages only if their rules allow links. Offer value first: share the webcams and road conditions page when people ask about conditions, and the Sawtell Peak guide when people ask about the road. Do not post the same link repeatedly.

## What not to do
- Do not buy links or join link-exchange schemes.
- Do not mass-email. A short, specific note to a real person works better.
- Do not put a cabin's exact address anywhere public.
