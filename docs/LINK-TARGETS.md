# Link targets from competitors' real backlinks

Source: OpenSEO backlink data pulled 2026-11-24 for islandparkidaho.com (192 links with domain rank 5+), islandparkchamber.org (89), pondslodge.com (47), visitislandparkidaho.com (38). Spam, "SEO cartel" and "links dealer" pages, pages.dev farms, and scraper sites were removed. "DR" is OpenSEO's domain rank of the linking site (0-100).

Rules: ask for a listing or offer a useful page. Never pay for a link. Never publish the cabin address. Only claim what our pages say. Send 3 to 5 a week. Contact details come from each site's own contact page; none were verified here.

## What this tells us

- islandparkidaho.com's best links are tourism boards (visittheusa network, visitidaho.org), travel blogs and old rental directories. Their strongest content links go to Johnny Sack Cabin, Big Springs and Island Park Reservoir, which we now have pages for.
- 495 referring domains, but most are spam. Ten to fifteen genuine links from Tier 1 and Tier 3 would put our link quality in the same range.
- The visittheusa/gousa network (about 10 DR 57-73 links) comes from one source: the Yellowstone Teton Territory feature. A listing or mention there is the single biggest prize.
