# Link and mention prospects (from OpenSEO backlink research, Nov 2026)

We looked at who links to the local sites that Google and AI answers cite most for Island Park searches:
islandparkidaho.com (88 dofollow links found) and islandparkchamber.org (34). DR = OpenSEO's domain rank (0-100) for the linking site.
Rule: earn links by being useful and by asking. Do not buy links or chase the thousands of auto-generated "pages.dev" pages in these profiles.

## 1. Official and association listings (best first)
| Prospect | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Island Park Area Chamber of Commerce (islandparkchamber.org) | The most cited local authority. Its listing is itself linked from yellowstoneteton.com and associate-chamber lists. Join and get a lodging listing. |
| Yellowstone Teton Territory (yellowstoneteton.com / .org) | The regional tourism board. Has listings pages (it lists the chamber) and a Visit USA "Idaho's Yellowstone Teton Territory" feature linked from ~10 national Visit USA sites (DR 57-73). Ask about a lodging listing. |
| Visit Idaho (visitidaho.org, DR 61) | State tourism site that links Island Park attractions. Check how lodging or itinerary content gets submitted. |
