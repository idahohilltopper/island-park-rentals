Best time to visit Island Park, Idaho

Short answer: Come in summer for Yellowstone, fishing and hiking, or in winter for snowmobiling, skiing and a snowy cabin with a hot tub. Island Park sits at about 6,300 feet, so mornings are crisp in every season and layers are always a good idea.

Temperatures by season

MonthAvg highAvg low
January26.5°F5.9°F
July77.1°F42.8°F

These are 1991 to 2020 NOAA normals for the Island Park Dam station. Annual precipitation averages about 28.8 inches, and snowfall tops 200 inches a year by published figures.

Summer: June to September

  • Cool nights and warm days, ideal for a drive into Yellowstone, hikes and Sawtell Peak.
  • Fishing: Yellowstone's season runs from the Saturday of Memorial Day weekend through October 31. See our fishing guides.
  • NPS projects West Entrance roads (West to Madison to Old Faithful) open April 16 through October 31, 2027, with the South and East entrances following in May. Dates can change.

Fall: September and October

Quieter roads, golden aspens and fall fishing. Yellowstone's main roads typically stay open through October 31, weather permitting.

Winter: December to March

  • Deep snow and groomed trails: see snowmobiling and winter in Island Park.
  • Harriman State Park lists over 33 miles of groomed Nordic trails. See the Harriman guide.
  • Yellowstone's guided snowmobile and snowcoach season is scheduled December 15, 2026 to March 15, 2027 through the West Entrance (dates can change).

Spring: April and May

Snow melts, rivers rise and park roads begin to open. Some Forest Service roads, like Sawtell Peak Road, open later. See road conditions and webcams before you travel.

Conditions and dates change, so check NPS park roads before you go. Not sure where to start? See our Island Park travel guide.
