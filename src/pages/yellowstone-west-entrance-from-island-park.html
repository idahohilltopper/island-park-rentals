Driving from Island Park to Yellowstone's West Entrance

Short answer: The West Entrance is at West Yellowstone, Montana, roughly 28 to 44 miles and 45 minutes to an hour from Island Park, Idaho, depending on where in Island Park you start. You take US Highway 20 north over Targhee Pass. The park's roads open to regular vehicles from mid-April, and in winter the entrance is for guided snowmobile and snowcoach trips.

The route

  • US Highway 20 north. Island Park's long "main street" is US 20, and the same highway carries you to West Yellowstone.
  • Targhee Pass. Wikipedia says US 20 crosses Targhee Pass on the Idaho-Montana border at 7,072 feet, about 15 miles west of West Yellowstone, and that it is the most direct access to the park from southern Idaho. Weather can change quickly at the top, so see our weather page and road conditions.
  • West Yellowstone. The town sits right at the entrance. The Grizzly and Wolf Discovery Center is there, and so are shops and restaurants.

Inside the park from the West Entrance

  • West Entrance to Madison Junction: about 30 minutes, per the National Park Service.
  • Madison Junction to Old Faithful: about 35 to 45 minutes. See our Yellowstone itinerary for a full day plan.
  • Plan on lines at busy times in summer, and leave early for the geyser basins.

When the West Entrance is open

SeasonWhat the National Park Service lists
SummerWest Entrance to Madison, Madison to Old Faithful and other park roads open April 16 to October 31, 2027. Dates change each year, and weather or projects can cause delays or closures.
WinterMost park roads are closed to regular vehicles from early November to late April. The West, South and East entrances are open to limited oversnow travel (commercially guided snowmobiles and snowcoaches) from December 15, 2026 to March 15, 2027.
Year-round roadThe only road generally open year-round to regular vehicles is from the North Entrance at Gardiner, Montana, through the park to Cooke City.

Source: the National Park Service park roads page, which lists a recorded road information line at (307) 344-2117. For roads on the Idaho side, see Idaho 511. Dates and hours change, so always check the park before you go.
