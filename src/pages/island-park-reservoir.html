Island Park Reservoir: boating and fishing guide

Short answer: Island Park Reservoir covers 8,400 acres on the Henrys Fork, surrounded by lodgepole pine. It is popular for boating, fishing and water skiing, with boat ramps and campgrounds around the shore.

What to do

  • Boat and water ski. The Forest Service lists ramps or docks at Buttermilk Campground, Island Park, Lakeside Lodge, McCrea Bridge Campground, Mill Creek and the West end.
  • Fish. Fishing is a listed activity. Check Idaho Fish and Game rules and license requirements before you go.
  • Camp. Campgrounds are listed at Buttermilk, McCrea Bridge, Mill Creek and West End.
  • Take the scenic drive. You can drive across the top of the dam for views of the Centennial Mountains and Box Canyon.

Plan your visit

  • The Forest Service says potable water is not available at the site, so bring your own.
  • Check for current water-quality advisories and campground dates with the Caribou-Targhee National Forest, 208-557-5900.
  • Fishing the Henrys Fork below the dam is a different experience. See Henrys Fork fly fishing.
