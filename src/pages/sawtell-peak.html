Sawtell Peak: road, views and mountain goats

Short answer: Sawtell Peak is a summit near Island Park that the Forest Service lists at about 9,866 feet. In summer you can drive a graded gravel road to the top for views of the Island Park caldera, Island Park Reservoir and Henrys Lake. We have photographed mountain goats on the rocks near the summit.

The drive

  • Road: Sawtell Peak Road (Forest Road 024) is well-graded gravel.
  • Turnoff: it turns west off US 20 about 1.5 miles north of Mack's Inn.
  • Season: the road is closed from about November 1 to June 1. Check current access with the Caribou-Targhee National Forest before you go.
  • Weather: conditions at the top can change fast, so bring layers even on a warm day.

What you can see

The Forest Service lists views of the Island Park caldera, Island Park Reservoir and Henrys Lake. For more on the lake, see our Henrys Lake guide and the Island Park Reservoir guide.

Mountain goats

We have photographed mountain goats on the rocky summit ridge, including a pair resting on the slope. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. Keep a safe distance, never feed or approach the animals, and use a zoom lens for close shots.

Plan your visit

  • Go on a clear day for the best views. The weather up top can change fast.
  • Fill the tank and bring water, since there are no services at the summit.
  • Combine it with Henrys Fork fishing, Big Springs or a drive to Mesa Falls.
  • See the Forest Service page: Sawtell Peak.

Conditions, access and closures change. Confirm with the Forest Service before you travel. Distances are approximate. See also our things to do in Island Park and the Island Park travel guide.
