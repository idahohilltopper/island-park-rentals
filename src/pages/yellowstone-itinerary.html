Yellowstone itinerary from Island Park: 2, 3 or 4 days

Old Faithful erupting in Yellowstone National Park. Photo: Yellowstone Gateway Stays.

Island Park puts you on the west side of Yellowstone, a short drive from the West Entrance. Use days 1 and 2 for the geyser basins and the Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone, day 3 for the north end of the park, and a fourth day to rest or explore Island Park itself. Drive times below come from the National Park Service and are averages, so build in extra time.

Drive times inside the park (National Park Service)

These are the NPS "average drive time" figures for common segments. The park's own page warns they "may be significantly longer due to wildlife on the roadway, weather, or infrastructure improvement projects." The speed limit is 45 mph unless posted otherwise.

Road segmentAverage drive time
West Entrance to Madison Junction30 minutes
Madison Junction to Old Faithful35 to 45 minutes
Norris Junction to Madison Junction30 minutes
Canyon Village to Norris Junction30 minutes
Mammoth Hot Springs to Norris Junction45 minutes
Canyon Village to Tower Junction50 to 60 minutes
Tower Junction to Mammoth Hot Springs40 minutes
Old Faithful to Grant Village40 minutes
Lake Village to Canyon Village35 minutes

Add the drive from Island Park to the West Entrance, roughly 30 to 45 minutes from our Whitebark Cabin. See getting to Island Park for distances from Bozeman, Big Sky and Idaho Falls.

2-day plan

  • Day 1, geysers: West Entrance to Madison Junction (about 30 minutes), then on to Old Faithful (35 to 45 minutes). Plan your day around an Old Faithful eruption, then explore the nearby geyser basins.
  • Day 2, Canyon: West Entrance to Madison Junction, Norris Junction and Canyon Village, using the segment times above (about 90 minutes one way). Visit the Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone, then return the same way.

3-day plan

  • Days 1 and 2: as above.
  • Day 3, north end: Madison Junction to Norris Junction (30 minutes) and on to Mammoth Hot Springs (45 minutes). From Mammoth, Tower Junction is another 40 minutes. This is a long day, so start early and expect to come back through the park.

4-day plan

  • Days 1 to 3: as above.
  • Day 4, Island Park: take a break from driving and explore close to the cabin. See things to do in Island Park, including Sawtell Peak, Henrys Fork fly fishing and Harriman State Park.

Old Faithful timing

The National Park Service reported a median interval of about 102 minutes between eruptions, plus or minus 10 minutes, with intervals from 54 to 118 minutes as of January 2025. Rangers post predictions at the Old Faithful Visitor Education Center and the lodges, and in the NPS app. They recommend arriving at least 10 minutes before the predicted time.

Wildlife and safety

  • Stay at least 100 yards from bears and wolves and at least 25 yards from all other animals, including bison and elk. See the NPS safety page.
  • Carry bear spray if you hike, and pull completely off the road to watch wildlife.
  • Fill up on gas and pack food and water. Services are spread out, and the NPS says to expect longer than usual driving times.

Visiting in winter?

This plan is built for Yellowstone's road season. Winter has its own wonderful version: guided snowmobile and snowcoach trips through the West Entrance (listed by the National Park Service from December 15, 2026 to March 15, 2027; dates can change), plus snowmobiling, skiing and hot tub evenings in Island Park. See winter in Island Park for a sample trip, and check the NPS Park Roads page for current dates.
