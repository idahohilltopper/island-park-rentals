Where to stay near Yellowstone: Island Park or West Yellowstone?

Short answer: Stay in West Yellowstone if you want to walk to restaurants and be minutes from the gate. Stay in Island Park if you want a private cabin, more space for a group, a hot tub and a quieter evening, in exchange for a drive into the park. Our cabin is about 38 miles from Yellowstone, roughly a 30 to 45 minute drive to the west entrance area.

Island Park vs West Yellowstone

Why groups pick Island Park

  • One cabin for up to 10 people instead of several hotel rooms.
  • A kitchen and laundry make longer stays easier and cheaper.
  • Pets are welcome at our cabin.
  • Fly fishing, hiking, ATV riding and snowmobiling start close to the door. See things to do.

Tips for the drive

  • Start early in summer, when park traffic and wildlife jams slow things down.
  • In winter, check road conditions before you go. Most park roads close to cars for the season, so plan winter park visits carefully.
  • Fill the tank and pack snacks. Services are sparse inside the park.
