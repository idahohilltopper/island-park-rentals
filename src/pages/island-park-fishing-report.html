Island Park fishing report: live Henrys Fork flows

Short answer: We are a cabin rental, not a fly shop, so we do not write daily fishing reports. What we can show is how much water is in the Henrys Fork right now, from USGS gauges, plus what anglers can expect by season and where to find reports from the guides who fish it every week.

Data: USGS Henrys Fork near Island Park, near Ashton and at St. Anthony. If the numbers do not load, use those links.

The Box Canyon gauge is below Island Park Dam, so it reflects what the dam is releasing. See our Island Park Reservoir guide and Henrys Fork fly fishing guide for what that means on the water. We do not interpret flows as good or bad fishing, so ask a guide or read a current report.

Where to find a current report

  • TroutHunter reports. The lodge and fly shop posts fishing reports written by its guides. They are posted irregularly, so check the date on each one.
  • River conditions. TroutHunter's river conditions page links flow pages for the upper and lower Henrys Fork, plus Henry's Fork Foundation tools for the daily report, water quality and hatch timing, and reservoir level and snowpack links.
  • Live river cam. TroutHunter also runs a live river cam.
  • Rules. Idaho Fish and Game for licenses, regulations and any closures.

We are not affiliated with TroutHunter. It is a local business we recommend. See our guide to choosing a fishing guide.

Seasons on the Henrys Fork

This is a summary of TroutHunter's seasons page. Hatches shift with water and weather, so treat the months as a guide.

SeasonMonthsHatches TroutHunter lists
SpringMarch to MayMidge, caddis, BWO, March brown, salmonfly, golden stone
SummerJune to AugustCaddis, BWO, PMD, green drake, brown drake, gray drake, trico, yellow sally, terrestrials
FallSeptember to NovemberTerrestrials, BWO, tricos, mahogany duns
WinterDecember to FebruaryMidges
  • TroutHunter says salmonflies arrive at the end of May or the first of June, and that terrestrial fishing starts by mid-July.
  • It says summer officially starts when the Harriman Ranch opens to fishing on June 15, and that Harriman closes to fishing on November 30. Idaho Parks and Recreation says fishing on the river closes starting December 1, 2026. See our Harriman guide.
