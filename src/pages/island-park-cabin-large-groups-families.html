A cabin for large groups and families near Yellowstone

Our Whitebark Cabin in Island Park, Idaho sleeps up to 10 in three bedrooms, with two full bathrooms, a full kitchen, a private hot tub and built-in air conditioning. It is about 38 miles from Yellowstone, so a family trip or a group of friends can share one place instead of booking several hotel rooms.

How a group of 10 fits

RoomBeds
Bedroom 1 (loft)2 queen beds and a sofa bed
Bedroom 2 (upstairs)1 queen bed
Bedroom 3 (upstairs)1 queen bed

There are two full bathrooms: one downstairs with a tub and laundry, and one upstairs with a shower. All three bedrooms are upstairs, so plan around that if anyone in your group has trouble with stairs.

What makes a shared cabin easier

  • A full kitchen and BBQ grill, so you can cook together after a day in the park.
  • A private hot tub with a meadow view, which the whole group can use after a long day.
  • Built-in air conditioning for summer.
  • An indoor fireplace and a backyard fire pit, when fire restrictions allow.
  • An in-unit washer and dryer, useful on longer stays.
  • Free parking, with space for small to medium trailers (under about 20 to 25 feet).
  • Pets welcome. Check the Airbnb listing for the current policy and fees.

Day trips for a group

  • Yellowstone. The west entrance area is about a 30 to 45 minute drive. Our Yellowstone itinerary has National Park Service drive times for 2, 3 or 4 days, and the West Entrance guide covers the route.
  • Mesa Falls. See Mesa Falls on the Henrys Fork, two waterfalls east of US 20.
  • Big Springs. Big Springs is the start of a National Recreation Water Trail, a lazy float of about three hours by Recreation.gov's description.
  • Fishing. The Henrys Fork is Island Park's best-known fishing river, and our fishing guide page explains how to choose a guide.
  • More ideas. See things to do in Island Park and the Grand Teton and Yellowstone itinerary.

Before you book

  • The cabin sleeps up to 10 guests. Confirm the guest limit, house rules, cancellation terms and fees on the Airbnb listing.
  • Island Park sits at about 6,400 feet at the cabin, so nights are cool even in summer. Pack layers. See the live weather page.
  • Buy groceries before you arrive or on the way. Our groceries and supplies guide lists nearby stores and fuel.
