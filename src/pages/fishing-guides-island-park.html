Fishing guides in Island Park, Idaho: how to choose

Short answer: A good guide shortens the learning curve on the Henrys Fork, Henrys Lake and nearby water. Before you book, check that the guide is licensed, ask what is included, and confirm who needs a fishing license. Island Park has several local fly shops and guide services to choose from.

What to check before you book

  • Guide license. Idaho licenses outfitters and guides through the Idaho Outfitters and Guides Licensing Board, which has a public license lookup. Ask the guide for their license and the outfitter they work for.
  • Your fishing license. Idaho Fish and Game says anyone 14 or older must have a valid fishing license to fish in Idaho. Its pages do not say whether a guided client is covered, so ask your guide. See Idaho Fish and Game licenses.
  • What is included. Gear, flies, lunch, shuttle, and trip length vary by guide.
  • Wade or float. Some water is best fished wading and some from a drift boat. Ask which fits your skill and your group.
  • Conditions and cancellations. River flows and weather change. Ask how the guide handles low or high water and what the cancellation terms are.

Where guides fish near Island Park

  • Henrys Fork fly fishing: Box Canyon and the Railroad Ranch at Harriman State Park.
  • Henrys Lake fishing.
  • Madison River, Buffalo and Warm rivers and the South Fork of the Snake.

Rules and seasons differ by section. Harriman State Park closes the river to fishing for the winter, and Yellowstone's fishing season runs from the Saturday of Memorial Day weekend through October 31. Check Idaho Fish and Game rules for the water you plan to fish.
