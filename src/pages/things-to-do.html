Things to do in Island Park, Idaho

Short answer: Island Park is a year-round outdoors destination. In warm months, fish the Henrys Fork or Henrys Lake, walk Harriman State Park, see Big Springs and Mesa Falls, and day-trip into Yellowstone. In winter, ride snowmobiles on groomed trails or ski and snowshoe at Harriman. Tap any photo below to learn more.

Fly fishing the Henrys Fork

The Henrys Fork of the Snake River runs through Island Park and is known among fly fishers worldwide. Box Canyon, just below Island Park Dam, is fast pocket water where wading can be hard; downstream the river slows through Harriman State Park. An Idaho fishing license is required. Check river flows and rules before you go.

Harriman State Park

Historic Railroad Ranch on the Henrys Fork, where moose, elk and trumpeter swans are commonly seen. In winter the park has groomed trails (roughly 25 miles by published figures) for classic and skate skiing, snowshoeing and fat biking.

Idaho Parks and Recreation ↗

Henrys Lake

A roughly 6,000-acre lake ringed by mountains along the Continental Divide, known for trout including Yellowstone cutthroat and brook trout. Henrys Lake State Park has a lakeshore campground and boat ramp, and the lake is close to our cabin.

Henrys Lake State Park ↗

Big Springs

A first-magnitude spring in the Caribou-Targhee National Forest and one of the two main sources of the Henrys Fork. Designated a National Natural Landmark in 1980. The Forest Service area has trails and picnic space and is popular in summer.

About Big Springs ↗

Mesa Falls

On the Henrys Fork east of US 20 and north of Ashton, Upper Mesa Falls drops about 114 feet and Lower Mesa Falls about 65 feet. Both are reached from the Mesa Falls Scenic Byway (Idaho Highway 47), roughly a 30-mile drive. Confirm current access with the Forest Service before you go.

Mesa Falls Scenic Byway ↗

Yellowstone National Park

Our cabin is about 38 miles from Yellowstone, roughly a 30 to 45 minute drive to the west entrance area. See how Island Park compares with West Yellowstone, and check road and entrance status with the National Park Service before you go.

Winter: snowmobiling and skiing

Island Park is one of the region's best-known snowmobiling areas. The Forest Service reports more than 500 miles of groomed trails across the Caribou-Targhee National Forest, and tourism sources cite more when nearby areas are included. Big Sky Resort is about an hour away; Jackson Hole and Grand Targhee are about two hours away.
