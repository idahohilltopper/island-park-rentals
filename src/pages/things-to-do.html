Things to do in Island Park, Idaho

Top picks: fly fishing the Henrys Fork of the Snake River, a day in Yellowstone, Mesa Falls, Harriman State Park, Henry's Lake and ATV or snowmobile riding. Our cabin is a comfortable base for all of them.

Fly fishing the Henrys Fork

The Henrys Fork of the Snake River runs through Island Park and is famous among fly fishers. Hire a local guide or check current regulations before you go.

Day trip to Yellowstone

The cabin is about 38 miles from Yellowstone. Read our guide to staying near Yellowstone.

Mesa Falls

The Mesa Falls Scenic Byway leads to Upper and Lower Mesa Falls, a popular stop for photos and short walks.

Harriman State Park

Harriman State Park offers wildlife viewing, trails and cross-country skiing in winter.

Henry's Lake

Henry's Lake is close to the cabin and popular for fishing and scenery.

ATV and UTV riding

The host offers ATV/UTV tours, listed at $170 per person. Ask on Airbnb for current details.

Winter

See our snowmobiling guide. Big Sky Resort is about an hour away, with Jackson Hole and Grand Targhee about two hours away.
