Airbnb in Island Park, Idaho: how to choose a cabin

Island Park is a cabin-rental town, so there are plenty of Airbnb options. The details that matter most are where the cabin sits along US 20, whether it has a hot tub and room for your group, whether pets and trailers are welcome, and what you plan to do in each season. Our Whitebark Cabin sleeps 10, welcomes pets and has a private hot tub.

What to look for in an Island Park Airbnb

  • Location along the highway. Island Park's main street is about 33 miles long, so a cabin near the Montana border and one near the south end are very different drives to Yellowstone. West Yellowstone is about 28 miles from the Yale-Kilgore Road junction. See our Island Park travel guide and Island Park vs West Yellowstone.
  • Sleeping setup. Count beds, not just the "sleeps" number, and check who gets the sofa bed or loft.
  • Amenities for the season. A hot tub and fireplace shine in winter. Air conditioning and a fire pit (when fire restrictions allow) matter in summer.
  • Pets and trailers. Policies vary by cabin. If you are bringing a dog, snowmobiles, or a small trailer, confirm it in the listing and with the host.
  • Reviews. Read recent reviews and the category scores, not only the headline rating.
  • House rules and fees. Cancellation terms, cleaning fees and rules are shown on Airbnb before you book. Read them.
