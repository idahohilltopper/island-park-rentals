What to expect in Island Park

Island Park is a forested, rural area on the Idaho side of the Yellowstone region, known for fly fishing on the Henrys Fork, lakes, trails and winter snowmobiling. Our Whitebark Cabin sits at about 6,400 feet, with crisp mountain air and four distinct seasons. Pack layers and enjoy it.

  • Plan your days: see our guides to staying near Yellowstone, things to do in Island Park, large groups and families and snowmobiling.
  • Booking and policies: house rules, cancellation terms and fees are shown on each Airbnb listing before you book.
  • Winter visits: Yellowstone has a special winter season of guided snowmobile and snowcoach trips. See the National Park Service's current dates on the National Park Service when you plan your trip.
