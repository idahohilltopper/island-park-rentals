About Yellowstone Gateway Stays

We're a small, owner-run rental business in Island Park, Idaho. We started coming here more than 20 years ago to go snowmobiling, and we fell in love with the area and the adventure.

How we got here

Island Park got us the way it gets a lot of people: on a snowmobile. We kept coming back, season after season, for the deep snow, the trails, the quiet forest and the feeling of being a short drive from Yellowstone and a long way from everything else.

Over those years we stayed in plenty of places that, unfortunately, weren't kept up the way they should be. That's what pushed us to do something about it. We wanted to offer a better place to stay, so we put our own cabins here.

What we want you to find

  • A cabin that's kept clean and cared for, so you can spend your trip outside instead of putting up with the rental.
  • Honest information. Every photo on this site is of the actual cabin, and our guides tell you what to expect before you go.
  • A real person to ask. The phone number on this site goes to us, the owners.
