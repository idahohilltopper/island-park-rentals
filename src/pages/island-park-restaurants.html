Where to eat in Island Park, Idaho

Short answer: Island Park is a spread-out resort town with a handful of restaurants and bars, many beside lakes and lodges. This guide is built from places we have eaten ourselves. We are starting with two favorites, Lakeside Lodge on Island Park Reservoir and Connie's Restaurant and Saloon, and we will add more over time.

Lakeside Lodge

Our pick: the tomahawk steak. We have had it with mushroom sauce, mashed potatoes and sauteed vegetables, and it is a good end to a day on the water or the trail.

  • Where: on Island Park Reservoir. The Forest Service lists Lakeside Lodge among the reservoir's boat ramp and dock locations. See our Island Park Reservoir guide.
  • What it is: the Island Park Chamber lists it under Lodging, Bar and Restaurants, with cabins, eight hotel-style rooms next to the main lodge, and RV and camping spaces.
  • Before you go: menus, hours and seasons change, so check the lodge's own site, lakesidelodgeandresort.com, or call.

We are not affiliated with Lakeside Lodge. This is simply a meal we enjoyed.

Connie's Restaurant and Saloon

Our picks: the Philly cheesesteak and the Zing Wings. They are casual, filling and easy after a day of riding, fishing or driving in the park.

  • What it is: the Island Park Chamber lists Connie's Restaurant and Saloon under its restaurants and bars. See its Chamber listing and its website, conniesrestaurant.org.
  • Before you go: menus, hours and seasonal items change, so call ahead or check its website for current details.

We are not affiliated with Connie's. These are meals we enjoyed.

Planning meals around a cabin stay

  • Island Park is spread along US 20, so check how far a restaurant is from your cabin before you head out.
  • Many guests cook some meals. Our Whitebark Cabin has a full kitchen, and you can stock up in town or at a larger grocery in a nearby town before you arrive.
  • In winter, hours can be shorter. Call ahead after a snowmobile or ski day.
  • For a full list of local restaurants and bars, see the Island Park Chamber of Commerce.
