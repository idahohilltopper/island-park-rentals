Island Park vs Big Sky vs West Yellowstone: where to stay

Short answer: Stay in West Yellowstone to be at the park gate and walk to restaurants. Stay in Big Sky if downhill skiing is the main goal. Stay in Island Park for a private cabin with room for a group, snowmobiling and a quieter forest setting. We rent cabins in Island Park, so we are not neutral, but the facts below come from published sources, not sales copy.

Choose Island Park if…

  • You want one place for a group of up to 10 instead of several hotel rooms (Whitebark Cabin).
  • You are traveling with a dog, since the Whitebark Cabin is pet friendly.
  • You plan to snowmobile, fish the Henrys Fork or explore Sawtell Peak, Mesa Falls and Henrys Lake. See things to do in Island Park.
  • You are happy to drive for park days. See the Yellowstone itinerary for drive times.

Choose Big Sky if…

  • Downhill skiing is the point of the trip. Big Sky Resort lists 5,850 skiable acres, 39 lifts, 320 named runs and a 4,350-foot vertical drop, with the Lone Peak Tram reaching the summit at 11,166 feet.
  • You want resort amenities and a short trip from Bozeman. Big Sky is roughly 45 to 50 miles south of Bozeman on US 191, about an hour to an hour and a quarter in good conditions.

Choose West Yellowstone if…

  • You want the shortest drive to the park and restaurants within walking distance.
  • You are joining a guided oversnow trip into Yellowstone in winter. West Yellowstone is a town of about 1,272 people (2020 census) at the West Entrance.

Distances between the three

Island Park to West Yellowstone is about 28 miles on US 20, and West Yellowstone to Big Sky is about 47 to 48 miles on US 191 through Gallatin Canyon. That makes Island Park to Big Sky roughly 75 miles as a rough estimate. See getting to Island Park from Bozeman, Big Sky and Idaho Falls for airport routes, and the Island Park vs West Yellowstone guide for winter park access.

Heading to Big Sky in winter? US 191 through Gallatin Canyon is the main route, and a snow-ready vehicle is a good idea, as with any mountain drive. Check road conditions before you go.
