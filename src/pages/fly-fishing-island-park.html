Fly fishing near Island Park, Idaho

Short answer: Island Park sits in the middle of some of the best trout water in the Rockies. Within a short drive you can fish Henrys Lake for trophy trout, the Henrys Fork of the Snake River, Island Park Reservoir, and the Madison River near West Yellowstone. Our Whitebark Cabin has trailer parking and a private hot tub for after the day on the water.

Other nearby streams include the Buffalo River and Warm River, and Fall River. Seasons, bag limits and special rules change, so always check the current regulations from Idaho Fish and Game, Montana Fish, Wildlife and Parks and the National Park Service.

Licenses in one minute

  • Idaho waters (Henrys Lake, Henrys Fork, Island Park Reservoir): an Idaho fishing license.
  • Montana waters on the Madison: a Montana fishing license.
  • Yellowstone National Park: a park fishing permit for anglers 16 and older. State licenses are not valid or required for park waters, according to the NPS.
