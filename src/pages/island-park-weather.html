Island Park, Idaho weather: live conditions and forecast

Short answer: Island Park sits at about 6,300 feet, so weather changes quickly and nights are cool even in summer. This page loads the latest nearby station reading and the National Weather Service forecast each time you open it, so check it the morning you drive up.

What to expect through the year

  • Cold winters, lots of snow. Wikipedia says the Island Park Dam weather station holds Idaho's record low of -60°F, from January 1943, and cites about 215 to 234 inches of snow a year. See our live snow report for current depths.
  • Cool nights in summer. At this elevation, evenings can be cool even in July. Pack a warm layer. See our elevation guide.
  • Plan by season. Our best time to visit guide covers each season, and our winter guide covers snowmobiling, skiing and getting around.
  • Roads and webcams. Check webcams and road conditions before you drive, and see our Yellowstone entrances page for winter access.
