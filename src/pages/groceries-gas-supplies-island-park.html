Groceries, gas and supplies in Island Park, Idaho

Short answer: Island Park has a few small grocery stores along its main road, plus fuel stops and a hardware store. The Island Park Chamber lists Sam Patch Co. as the largest and most complete grocery store in town. If you are cooking at a cabin, plan a quick stop for the basics and stock up in a larger town for a big shop.

Groceries

  • Sam Patch Co. Grocery and Sundries. The Chamber calls it the largest and most complete grocery store in Island Park, with fresh produce, meats, dairy, frozen foods, snacks and pantry staples, plus toiletries and camping supplies. It is on Big Springs Loop Road, next to a Chevron-branded fuel station, and the Chamber says it also provides propane and campground-friendly essentials. Website: sampatchco.com.
  • Henry's Lake Station. The Chamber describes it as a truck stop with fuel, a cafe and a convenience store.
  • Last Chance General Store and Texaco. On the bank of the Henrys Fork along Highway 20, per the Chamber's listing.

Hardware and supplies

  • Ace Hardware. The store page is linked here. We could not load it, so we have not confirmed what the store carries. Call before you go if you need something specific, such as propane, firewood or fishing gear.

Tips for a cabin stay

  • Our Whitebark Cabin has a full kitchen and an in-unit washer and dryer, which makes longer stays easier.
  • Island Park stores are small, so bring specialty items with you, and buy a big shop in a larger town on the way if you need one.
  • Winter hours can be shorter. Call ahead after a snowmobile or ski day. See winter in Island Park.
