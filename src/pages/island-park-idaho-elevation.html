Island Park, Idaho elevation: how high is it?

Short answer: Island Park sits at about 6,300 feet. Wikipedia calls it the highest city in Idaho, and the nearby high country runs from about 7,700 feet at the White Elephant snow station to nearly 9,900 feet at Sawtell Peak.

Sources differ by a few feet, so treat these as approximate. Wikipedia also says the floor of the Henrys Fork Caldera, the large crater that holds Island Park, is at about 6,000 feet.

What the elevation means for your visit

  • Snow. Wikipedia cites average snowfall of about 215 to 234 inches a year, which is why Island Park is a winter destination. See our live snow report for current depths from NRCS SNOTEL stations, including the Island Park station.
  • Cooler nights. At this elevation, evenings can be cool even in summer, so pack layers. See our best time to visit guide.
  • Altitude. Some visitors notice the altitude on the first day. Drinking water and taking it easy at first is common advice. If you have a health condition, ask your doctor before you travel.
  • The high country. The road to the top of Sawtell Peak climbs to nearly 9,900 feet, so conditions up high can differ a lot from town.
