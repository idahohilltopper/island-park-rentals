Henrys Lake fishing: trophy trout near Island Park

Short answer: Henrys Lake is a roughly 6,000-acre lake ringed by mountains along the Continental Divide, and Idaho Fish and Game describes it as one of the best places in Idaho to catch trophy trout. It holds Yellowstone cutthroat, brook trout and cutthroat-rainbow hybrids, and it is a popular ice fishing destination in winter. It is a short drive from Island Park and from Yellowstone's West Entrance.

What you can catch

  • Yellowstone cutthroat trout, the native species.
  • Brook trout, including fish big enough to set state records. Idaho Fish and Game reported two state-record brook trout in a single December.
  • Cutthroat-rainbow hybrids ("cutbows"), sterile fish stocked to provide trophy fishing while protecting native cutthroat genetics.

Seasons, limits and special rules

Idaho Fish and Game changed the Henrys Lake season in recent years, extending the fishing opportunity while keeping a two-trout limit across all trout species. Separate rules apply to the lake's tributaries, and some areas near Staley Springs and Hatchery Creek have been closed to fishing. Because rules change, use the current Henrys Lake page on the Idaho Fishing Planner and the annual rulebook. You need an Idaho fishing license. Idaho Fish and Game also publishes Henrys Lake newsletters with fishery updates.

Ice fishing

Ice fishing is a major draw at Henrys Lake, and Idaho Fish and Game has reported strong winter catches in past seasons. Ice thickness and conditions vary, so confirm safety before you go out. See winter in Island Park for other winter activities.

Henrys Lake State Park

Henrys Lake State Park has a lakeshore campground and a boat ramp. See our camping in Island Park guide for other campground options.
