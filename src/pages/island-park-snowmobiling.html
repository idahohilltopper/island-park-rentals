Island Park snowmobiling: plan a winter trip

Quick take: Island Park is one of the best-known snowmobiling destinations in the Rockies, with hundreds of miles of trails through national forest country. Our cabin has room for small to medium trailers, a private hot tub for after the ride, and the host offers discounted snowmobile rentals.

Why ride from Island Park

  • Deep snow and extensive trail systems close to town.
  • Easy to combine riding with a visit to the area's winter scenery.
  • Gear dry-out, laundry and a full kitchen for group trips.

Cabin features for riders

  • Parking for small to medium trailers (under about 20 to 25 feet).
  • Private hot tub, indoor fireplace and in-unit laundry.
  • Sleeps up to 10, so one cabin works for a whole riding group.
  • Pets welcome.

Snowmobiling in Yellowstone

Yellowstone National Park's winter oversnow season is separate from Island Park's trails. The National Park Service schedules the West Entrance, at West Yellowstone, to open to guided snowmobiles and snowcoaches from December 15, 2026 to March 15, 2027, with dates that can change. Check the NPS snowmobiling page for current rules, guides and entrance status.

Other winter activities

Cross-country skiing is available at nearby Harriman State Park, while Big Sky Resort is a longer drive (about 47 to 48 miles north of West Yellowstone) and Jackson Hole is roughly 100 miles away. See our winter in Island Park guide.

Ride smart

Check trail and avalanche reports, ride with a group and dress in layers, as local riders do.
