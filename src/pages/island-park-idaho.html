Island Park, Idaho: your travel guide

Short answer: Island Park is a forested, lake-and-river country in eastern Idaho, about 28 miles from West Yellowstone and Yellowstone's West Entrance. It sits inside one of the world's largest calderas, stretches along a roughly 33-mile "main street" (US Highway 20), and is known for fly fishing, heavy winter snow and easy access to Yellowstone and Grand Teton. Our Whitebark Cabin sleeps 10 and has a private hot tub.

SnowPublished averages range from about 154 to 219 inches a year, making it one of Idaho's snowiest communities

Things to do by season

  • Summer: fish the Henrys Fork, Henrys Lake and Madison River, drive up Sawtell Peak, visit Mesa Falls, Big Springs and Harriman State Park, and day-trip into Yellowstone. See things to do in Island Park.
  • Fall: a favorite for anglers, with cooler water on the Madison and the Henrys Fork. See the Madison River guide.
  • Winter: snowmobiling, cross-country skiing, ice fishing and hot tub evenings. See winter in Island Park and the snowmobiling guide.
  • Spring: quieter trails and rivers, with fishing on spring-fed streams such as the Warm River when other waters run high.
