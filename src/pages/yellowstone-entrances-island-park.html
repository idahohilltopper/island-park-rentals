Yellowstone entrances: which is closest to Island Park?

Short answer: The West Entrance, at West Yellowstone, Montana, is the closest to Island Park, Idaho. Published distances run roughly 28 to 44 miles, about 45 minutes to an hour, depending on where in Island Park you start. Yellowstone has five entrances, and several are closed to regular vehicles in winter.

The National Park Service says Yellowstone has five entrance stations, and several are closed to regular vehicles during winter. See the NPS directions page.

Why most Island Park visitors use the West Entrance

  • It is the shortest drive from Island Park, and the road passes through West Yellowstone.
  • From the West Entrance, the National Park Service lists about 30 minutes to Madison Junction, then 35 to 45 minutes on to Old Faithful. See our Yellowstone itinerary.
  • The Grizzly and Wolf Discovery Center is right at the entrance in West Yellowstone.

Yellowstone entrances in winter

  • The road from the North Entrance at Gardiner to the Northeast Entrance stays open to cars.
  • The National Park Service schedules the West Entrance to open to guided snowmobiles and snowcoaches from December 15, 2026 to March 15, 2027, with dates that can change.
  • Check the park's current road and winter information before you plan. See also our winter guide and the West Yellowstone snow report.

Spring and fall

The National Park Service says most roads open mid-April through October 31, and that weather and improvement projects can cause delays or closures. Check NPS plan your visit pages and our webcams and road conditions page before you go.

Getting here

For the West Entrance and Island Park, Bozeman and Idaho Falls are the main year-round airport choices. See the closest airport to Yellowstone and our guide to getting to Island Park.
