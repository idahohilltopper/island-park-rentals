Mack's Inn, Island Park, Idaho

Short answer: Mack's Inn is a landmark stop on US Highway 20 in Island Park, about 22 miles south of West Yellowstone. It is known for a leisurely 5-mile float on the Henrys Fork, with rafts, kayaks, canoes, paddleboards and tubes, plus lodging and The Parlor, a pizza and ice cream restaurant. It is also the end of the Big Springs Water Trail.

Float the Henrys Fork

  • The float. Mack's Inn River Adventures runs a leisurely 5-mile float on the North Fork of the Snake River, the Henrys Fork. Its site says guests have seen moose, elk, deer, trout, kokanee salmon in the fall, eagles, swans and more.
  • What you can rent. Rafts, tandem and single kayaks, canoes, stand-up paddleboards and river tubes. Rentals are 3 hours, and a life jacket and transportation to the launch site are included.
  • Season. The official site says open Memorial Day Weekend through Labor Day. Launches are cancelled in bad weather.
  • Rules. The site says pets and fishing equipment are not permitted on rafts.
  • Booking. Reservations and prices are on the Mack's Inn River Adventures page.

Eat: The Parlor at Mack's Inn

The Island Park Chamber says The Parlor serves wood-fired pizzas, creamy ice cream and casual dishes. See its Chamber listing and website for current details. More in where we eat in Island Park.

Stay

The official site describes a historic log motel built in 1949 and, since 2020, a 111-room hotel nearby. Book through macksinn.com. If you want a whole cabin for a group, see our Whitebark Cabin, which sleeps 10 and has a private hot tub.

Where it is and what is nearby

  • On US 20. Recreation.gov says Mack's Inn is 22 miles south of West Yellowstone, Montana, on Highway 20.
  • Big Springs. Turn east on Big Springs Loop Road at Mack's Inn and drive about 4.5 miles to the Big Springs area. The Big Springs Water Trail ends at Mack's Inn about 4.5 miles from where it starts. Recreation.gov's listing says Mack's Inn offers boat rentals, rafting, shuttle service, gas, groceries, showers and laundry. See our Big Springs guide and Johnny Sack Cabin.
  • Sawtell Peak. The Forest Service says Sawtell Peak Road turns west off Highway 20 about 1.5 miles north of Mack's Inn. See our Sawtell Peak guide.
  • Fishing. The Henrys Fork runs through here. See our live flows and Henrys Fork guide.

A bit of history

Mack's Inn has been a stop for travelers in Island Park for a long time. A University of Idaho postcard collection includes a 1930 postcard of Mack's Inn among the pines, and the official site dates the current log motel to 1949.

Hours, seasons and prices change, so confirm with each business. We are not affiliated with Mack's Inn. Sources: macksinn.com, the Island Park Chamber, Recreation.gov, the Forest Service and the University of Idaho Library.
