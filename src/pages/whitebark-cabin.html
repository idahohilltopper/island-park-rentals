The cabin sits in a forested area of Island Park, Idaho, about 38 miles from Yellowstone National Park and near Henry's Lake and Island Park's trail system. The exact address is shared after booking. See how Island Park compares with West Yellowstone and what to do nearby.

  • Hot tub and BBQ servicing happens weekly. Check the listing for details.
