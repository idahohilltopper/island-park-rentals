Island Park and Yellowstone webcams and road conditions

Short answer: Check the official sources below before you head out. They give you live camera views, road status, trail reports and park conditions in one place, so you can plan the best day for your trip. Everything here links to the agency that publishes it.

Yellowstone webcams

  • NPS Yellowstone webcams: the National Park Service lists eight webcams with views across the park, including a live view of Old Faithful and the Upper Geyser Basin and a West Entrance camera at West Yellowstone, Montana.

Roads and park access

  • NPS Park Roads: seasonal opening and closing dates for park roads and entrances, including winter oversnow dates.
  • NPS Current Conditions: what is happening in the park right now.
  • Idaho 511: road conditions and cameras for Idaho highways, including US 20 through Island Park.
  • Montana 511: road conditions for Montana highways such as US 191 and the road into West Yellowstone.
  • Wyoming road conditions: for Jackson and Grand Teton trips.

Winter: trail reports and conditions

  • Destination Yellowstone trail reports: regional snowmobile trail reports.
  • Harriman State Park: winter trail information for skiing, snowshoeing and fat biking.
  • Caribou-Targhee National Forest: forest conditions and closures.

See winter in Island Park and the snowmobiling guide for how to plan a winter trip.

Fishing conditions

River flows and closures change daily in summer. Use the NPS fishing page and Montana waterbody closures, plus Idaho Fish and Game. See our fishing hub.
