Why we're in Island Park

We started coming to Island Park more than 20 years ago to go snowmobiling, and we fell in love with the area and the adventure.

Over the years we stayed in a lot of places that, unfortunately, weren't kept up the way they should be. So we decided to change that and give people a better place to stay. Our cabins are kept clean and cared for, so you can spend your trip enjoying Island Park and Yellowstone country.
