Closest airport to Yellowstone: which should you fly into?

There is no single closest airport, because it depends on which Yellowstone entrance you use. The National Park Service says airlines serve Cody and Jackson (Wyoming), Bozeman and Billings (Montana) and Idaho Falls (Idaho) year-round. For the West Entrance and Island Park, the practical choices are Bozeman and Idaho Falls.

Distances come from the National Park Service, published route estimates and local guides, and they vary by a few miles with the starting point. Check a live map before you drive.

Most popular airports

A National Park Service visitor survey found the most popular arrival airports before entering Yellowstone were Jackson Hole (21%), Salt Lake City (20%), Bozeman Yellowstone International (14%) and Denver (12%).

Best airport for Island Park, Idaho

  • Idaho Falls: the most direct route. US 20 north through Rexburg, St. Anthony and Ashton reaches Island Park in roughly 1 hour 20 to 1 hour 30 minutes.
  • Bozeman: more flight options. Drive US 191 south to West Yellowstone (about 90 miles), then US 20 south about 28 miles into Island Park. See getting to Island Park for the full route.
  • Jackson: useful if you are pairing Yellowstone with Grand Teton. See the Grand Teton and Yellowstone itinerary.

Flying in for winter?

Guided snowmobile and snowcoach trips into Yellowstone start from towns like West Yellowstone, and Idaho Falls and Bozeman both work well as gateways. See Island Park vs West Yellowstone and winter in Island Park.
