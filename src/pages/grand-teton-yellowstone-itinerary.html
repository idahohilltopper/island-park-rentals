Grand Teton and Yellowstone itinerary from Island Park

The Teton Range from the Centennial Mountains near Island Park. Photo: Yellowstone Gateway Stays.

Island Park is a good base for Yellowstone's west side and sits roughly 100 miles (about two hours or more) from Jackson and Grand Teton National Park. Give Yellowstone two or three days, Grand Teton one or two, and a rest day in Island Park. Drive times below come from the National Park Service or published route estimates, so allow extra time.

A 5-day outline

  • Day 1, Yellowstone geysers: West Entrance to Madison Junction (about 30 minutes), then Old Faithful (35 to 45 minutes), per the National Park Service.
  • Day 2, Yellowstone Canyon: West Entrance to Madison Junction, Norris Junction and Canyon Village (about 30 minutes for each segment). See the full Yellowstone itinerary.
  • Day 3, drive to Grand Teton: Island Park to Jackson is roughly 100 miles and about 2 hours to 2 hours 20 minutes by published estimates. Spend the afternoon at Jackson Lake and Moose.
  • Day 4, Grand Teton: Jenny Lake and its trails, then Mormon Row and Menors Ferry near Moose.
  • Day 5, Island Park: a day without a long drive. See things to do in Island Park.

Another option is a day trip from Island Park. It makes for a long day, so many visitors give Grand Teton its own overnight. Staying in Island Park for the Yellowstone days and moving for the Grand Teton days keeps the driving down.

Grand Teton highlights (National Park Service)

  • Jenny Lake: one of the most-visited areas, with trails to Hidden Falls (5 miles round trip from the visitor center, or about 1 mile from the west boat dock) and Inspiration Point (6 miles round trip, or about 2 miles from the west dock using the shuttle boat). Parking is often full from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., so go early or late.
  • Jackson Lake: Jackson Lake Dam raises the lake a maximum of 39 feet, and the Signal Mountain summit road has overlooks of the Teton Range and Jackson Lake.
  • Moose: the Craig Thomas Discovery and Visitor Center, plus the historic Mormon Row district with the Moulton barns and Menors Ferry.
  • Teton Park Road: the Jenny Lake Trailhead is eight miles north of Moose Junction on this road.

The National Park Service warns of construction in Grand Teton, so check the park's places-to-go page for current conditions before you plan.

Combining the parks

  • Yellowstone's South Entrance leads toward Grand Teton. The NPS lists South Entrance to Grant Village at 50 minutes and Old Faithful to Grant Village at 40 minutes.
  • Visiting in winter? Yellowstone's winter season brings guided snowmobile and snowcoach trips. See winter in Island Park.
  • For a longer trip with ski days, compare Island Park, Big Sky and West Yellowstone.
