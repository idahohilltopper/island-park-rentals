Grizzly and Wolf Discovery Center: plan your visit

Short answer: The Grizzly and Wolf Discovery Center is an AZA-accredited, not-for-profit wildlife park next to Yellowstone's West Entrance in West Yellowstone, Montana. It is a good rainy-day or wildlife-viewing stop, about 28 miles from Island Park. Check the official site for current hours and prices, because both change.

What you will see

The center's residents were removed from the wild or rescued. Its wildlife includes grizzly bears, gray wolves, river otters and birds of prey. It also works as an education facility about living safely with bears in the Yellowstone region.

Hours and tickets

  • Hours change with the seasons. Listings describe roughly 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. with last entry at 5:30 p.m. outside summer, and longer evening hours from mid-May to early September.
  • Prices differ between sources, so we do not quote a number here. Check the official site, grizzlydiscoveryctr.org, for admission and any combination tickets.
  • One listing says admission is good for two consecutive days. Confirm this when you buy.

Fitting it in from Island Park

West Yellowstone is about 28 miles from the Yale-Kilgore Road junction in Island Park on US 20, roughly 30 to 40 minutes, and our Whitebark Cabin is about 38 miles from Yellowstone National Park. Pair the center with a park day or an afternoon in West Yellowstone. See the Yellowstone itinerary for NPS drive times, and Island Park vs West Yellowstone to decide where to stay.

Wildlife safety in the wild

The National Park Service asks visitors to stay at least 100 yards from bears and wolves and 25 yards from other animals. See the NPS safety page.
