Camping in Island Park, Idaho

Short answer: Island Park has national forest and state park campgrounds along the Henrys Fork, Island Park Reservoir and Henrys Lake. Opening dates, reservation rules and construction closures change from year to year, so confirm each campground before you plan. If you would rather have a roof and a hot tub, our cabin is a few minutes from the same trails and water.

Campgrounds people ask about

  • McCrea Bridge (Caribou-Targhee National Forest): on the backwaters of Island Park Reservoir, about 29 miles southwest of West Yellowstone. One guide says it typically opens in late May and operates through early September, and that reservoir access can become difficult by August. Reservation status is described differently by different sources, so check its Recreation.gov page.
  • Buffalo Campground: along the Buffalo River, which is fed by a large spring about 4.5 miles away, and close to Yellowstone, Henrys Lake State Park and Harriman State Park. See Recreation.gov.
  • Henrys Lake State Park: lakeshore campsites on a roughly 6,000-acre lake surrounded by the Continental Divide, with a boat ramp. See Idaho Parks and Recreation.
  • Big Springs, Upper Coffee Pot and Buttermilk are also listed. Big Springs Campground has been affected by construction, so verify its status.

For a longer list, see Visit Idaho's guide to 14 Idaho campgrounds near Yellowstone.

Planning tips

  • Opening and closing dates change each year, and sites can fill early in summer.
  • At roughly 6,000 to 7,000 feet, nights can be cold even in summer. Pack warm layers.
  • Follow campfire rules and fire restrictions, which can change quickly.
