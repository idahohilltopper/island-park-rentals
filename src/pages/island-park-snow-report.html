How to read the numbers

  • Snow depth is the inches of snow on the ground at the station.
  • Snow water equivalent (SWE) is the inches of water the snowpack would make if it melted. A high SWE for the depth means dense, well-settled snow.
  • Change compares the newest reading with 24 hours and 7 days earlier, so you can see recent storms at a glance.
  • Temperature is the air temperature at the station when the reading was taken.

These are point readings from the high country. Conditions in town, on groomed trails and on the roads can differ, so use this as a guide, not a trail report. For grooming, riding conditions and road status, see our webcams and road conditions page and the Island Park Chamber.
