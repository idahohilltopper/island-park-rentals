Island Park, Idaho hotels vs cabins: where to stay

Short answer: Island Park has a handful of hotels, lodges and condominiums, plus many cabin rentals. Pick a hotel or lodge room for a short, simple stay. Pick a cabin if you have a group, want a kitchen, or are coming in winter. Either way, you are a short drive from Yellowstone's West Entrance.

What kinds of lodging Island Park has

The Island Park Area Chamber of Commerce lodging directory lists cabin rentals, motels and lodges, homes and condos, campgrounds and RV sites. Examples named there include:

  • Lakeside Lodge: one- and two-bedroom cabins and a small number of hotel rooms, per its listing.
  • Sawtelle Mountain Resort: from standard hotel rooms to cabins, a group-friendly bunkhouse and RV or tent sites, per its listing.
  • Springhill Suites by Marriott: described by the Chamber as just minutes from the West Entrance.
  • The Pines: log cabin rentals, per its listing.
  • Timbers Condominiums: condominiums the Chamber places 22 miles from the West Entrance.
  • Eagle Ridge Ranch: rustic cabins, per its listing.

We are not affiliated with these businesses. Offerings change, so confirm details with each one.
