The stations

  • West Yellowstone (Gallatin County, Montana, 6,680 feet). The station NRCS names for the town, and the best comparison for in-town snow.
  • Whiskey Creek (Gallatin County, Montana, 6,790 feet).
  • Madison Plateau (Gallatin County, Montana, 7,710 feet). The highest of the stations closest to town.
  • Black Bear (Gallatin County, Montana, 8,160 feet). A higher station south of town. See its 7-day history.

Station names, counties and elevations are from the NRCS station metadata.

Yellowstone in winter

The National Park Service schedules the West Entrance, at West Yellowstone, to open to guided snowmobiles and snowcoaches from December 15, 2026 to March 15, 2027, with dates that can change. Check the park's current schedule before you plan, and see our winter guide for how a cabin base in Island Park fits with a day in the park.

Staying in Island Park

Published distances from Island Park to West Yellowstone range from about 33 to 44 miles, and drive times run roughly 45 minutes to an hour depending on where in Island Park you start. Many visitors ride Island Park's groomed trails one day and head into the park with a guide the next. See our Island Park snow report for the Idaho stations, Island Park snowmobiling, sled rentals and webcams and road conditions.

Our Whitebark Cabin sleeps 10 and has a private hot tub for after a day in the snow.

How to read the numbers

  • Snow depth is inches of snow on the ground at the station.
  • Snow water equivalent is how many inches of water the snowpack would make if it melted.
  • Change compares the newest reading with 24 hours and 7 days earlier.

These are point readings, not trail or road reports. Conditions can differ in town and on the roads.
