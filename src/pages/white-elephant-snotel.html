About the station

  • Name and ID: White Elephant, SNOTEL 860
  • Location: Fremont County, Idaho, about 44.53267°N, 111.41085°W
  • Elevation: 7,670 feet
  • Records begin: October 1979
  • Operator: USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service

Location, elevation and start date are from the NRCS station metadata.

How to read the numbers

  • Snow depth is inches of snow on the ground at the station.
  • Snow water equivalent is how many inches of water the snowpack would make if it melted. It shows how dense the snow is.
  • Precipitation accumulation is the running total of precipitation measured by the station.
  • Air temperature is the temperature when the reading was taken.
