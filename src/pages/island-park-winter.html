Winter in Island Park, Idaho

Short answer: Winter is Island Park's big season for snowmobiling, with more than 500 miles of groomed trails in the surrounding national forest. Add cross-country skiing and snowshoeing at Harriman State Park, ice fishing on Island Park Reservoir and a private hot tub at our Whitebark Cabin for the evenings. Snow and road conditions change, so check before you travel.

Snowmobiling

Island Park is one of the best-known snowmobiling areas in the region. The Forest Service reports more than 500 miles of groomed trails across the Caribou-Targhee National Forest, and tourism sources cite higher totals when nearby areas are included. Grooming happens as weather, snow and safety permit, and local sources describe a riding season that usually runs through March. Check current conditions on the regional trail reports page and the Idaho snowmobile program. Our live snow report, snowmobiling guide covers trailer parking at the cabin and rentals.

Cross-country skiing, snowshoeing and fat biking

Harriman State Park has groomed winter trails, roughly 25 miles by published figures, for classic and skate skiing, snowshoeing and fat biking. Check Idaho Parks and Recreation for current grooming and conditions.

Ice fishing

Island Park Reservoir is popular for ice fishing in winter, according to published fishing guides. Ice thickness and conditions vary, so confirm safety before you go out and check Idaho Fish and Game rules.

Yellowstone in winter

Yellowstone is a different park in winter, snow-covered and quiet, and there are great ways to see it. For 2026 to 2027 the National Park Service lists guided snowmobile and snowcoach trips through the West Entrance from December 15, 2026 to March 15, 2027 (dates can change). The road from the North Entrance at Gardiner to the Northeast Entrance also stays open to cars. Island Park is about 28 miles from West Yellowstone, the classic starting point for guided trips. See the NPS Park Roads page and our Island Park vs West Yellowstone guide.

Downhill skiing

Big Sky Resort is the major ski area on the Montana side of the region, with 5,850 skiable acres and a 4,350-foot vertical drop by its own figures. It is roughly 75 miles from Island Park by road as a rough estimate (about 28 miles to West Yellowstone plus about 47 to 48 miles to Big Sky). Jackson Hole is roughly 100 miles away. Compare the options in Island Park vs Big Sky vs West Yellowstone.

Stay warm: the cabin

  • Private hot tub and an indoor fireplace
  • Parking for small to medium trailers (under about 20 to 25 feet)
  • In-unit laundry for wet gear, and a full kitchen
  • Sleeps up to 10 and welcomes pets

Check webcams, road conditions and trail reports before you head out.

A sample winter trip

  • Day 1: Arrive, settle in and finish the day in the hot tub.
  • Day 2: Ride Island Park's groomed snowmobile trails. The Whitebark Cabin's host offers discounted snowmobile rentals, and there is trailer parking if you bring your own sled.
  • Day 3: Take a guided snowcoach or snowmobile trip into Yellowstone from West Yellowstone (December 15 to March 15 in 2026 to 2027, per the National Park Service; book with a licensed guide).
  • Day 4: Ski or snowshoe at Harriman State Park, or try the ride to Mesa Falls, then warm up by the fireplace.

Make the most of winter

  • Pack layers. Island Park sits around 7,000 feet, with crisp mountain air and plenty of snow.
  • A snow-ready vehicle (winter tires, chains or all-wheel drive) makes the drive easy, and it never hurts to check road conditions first.
  • Do what local riders do: check trail reports and avalanche forecasts, and ride with a group.
