Where to rent snowmobiles and side-by-sides in Island Park and West Yellowstone

Island Park, Idaho and West Yellowstone, Montana both have outfitters that rent snowmobiles in winter and ATVs or side-by-sides in summer, and some of them let you ride straight onto the trail system. Prices, seasons and requirements change, so we link to each business instead of quoting them. Book early for winter weekends.

Island Park, Idaho West Yellowstone, Montana

Our cabin is in Island Park, Idaho. West Yellowstone is the Montana town at the park's west entrance, and it has its own rental shops near the trails. We list both so you can compare.

Want a quick side-by-side comparison? See the powersports rentals table.

In the town of Island Park, Idaho

Snowmobile rentals

  • Island Park Backcountry Rentals (Yale-Kilgore Road). Snowmobile-only shop with Ski-Doo and Polaris sleds. Its site says you can ride directly from the rental location onto the trail system, that helmets are required, and that helmet and gear rental is available.
  • Island Park Rentals. Ski-Doo snowmobiles, listed for late December to March, with pickup just off the Island Park golf course and close trail access.
  • Island Park Expeditions (Blue Buffalo Resort, US 20). Lists snowmobile, ATV, UTV and e-bike rentals year-round, with equipment depending on the season. Its Chamber listing says rentals come with necessary safety and trail equipment.
  • High Mountain Adventures (Sawtell Peak Road). Polaris snowmobile rentals at the base of Sawtell Peak. Its site advertises over 500 miles of groomed trails and suggests calling for same-day availability. They also run the live camera we link to.
  • Lakeside Lodge and Resort. Snowmobile rentals with access directly from the lodge, on Island Park Reservoir.

Side-by-side and ATV rentals

  • Island Park Rentals. Can-Am ATVs and UTVs, listed for May to November, with pickup just off the golf course.
  • Island Park Expeditions. ATVs, UTVs and side-by-sides at Blue Buffalo Resort.
  • Lakeside Lodge and Resort. Summer side-by-side UTVs. The lodge's site mentions a discount on motorized rentals for lodge guests.
  • Island Park Adventures (Big Springs Loop). A dealer whose site lists side-by-side, ATV and watercraft rentals booked through an online reservation page.

Island Park Rentals also rents boats, pontoons and jet skis in summer if you want a day on the reservoir.

In West Yellowstone, Montana

West Yellowstone is the Montana gateway town at the park's west entrance, with a large groomed snowmobile trail system in winter. Several shops sit near the trails, and some let you ride from the door.

Snowmobile rentals

  • Highmark Rentals (633 Madison Ave). Rents Polaris and Ski-Doo snowmobiles, including trail, crossover and mountain models. Its site says the shop is 150 feet from the main trailhead with no trailering required, and it uses a top-off-the-tank fuel policy.
  • Yellowstone Adventures (Dunraven St). Guided tours and self-guided snowmobile rentals, and a Ski-Doo dealer.
  • Travelers Snowmobile Rentals (Electric St). Snowmobile rentals; see its Polaris Adventures listing.

Side-by-side and ATV rentals

  • Highmark Rentals. Summer ATV and UTV rentals, with Polaris RZR and Can-Am Defender models listed.
  • Yellowstone Adventures. Two- and four-seat Polaris side-by-sides listed for the summer season. Its site lists age limits for renters, so check them before you plan a family trip.
  • Travelers Snowmobile Rentals. Its Polaris Adventures listing also mentions side-by-side rentals.

What to ask before you book

  • What is included: helmet, gear, fuel, insurance or a damage waiver, trailer.
  • Age and license rules for drivers and passengers.
  • Whether you ride from the shop or need to trailer the machine to the trailhead.
  • Snow depth and grooming dates in winter, and the deposit and cancellation terms.
  • How many seats you need. Side-by-sides come in two-, four- and six-seat sizes at some shops.

Before you ride

  • Summer: see our side-by-side guide for Idaho OHV rules, trail widths and the Forest Service map.
  • Check conditions and webcams before you head out.
