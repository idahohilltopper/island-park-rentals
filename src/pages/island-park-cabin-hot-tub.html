Island Park, Idaho cabin with a private hot tub

Our Whitebark Cabin in Island Park, Idaho, has a private hot tub, an indoor fireplace and a backyard fire pit. It sleeps up to 10, welcomes pets and is a short drive from Yellowstone. The Airbnb listing says the hot tub is serviced weekly. See the listing on Airbnb for details.

Soak after a winter day

Island Park is a winter playground. Guests ride snowmobiles on groomed trails, ski at Harriman State Park and snowshoe through the pines, then come home to a hot tub. NOAA's January normals for the Island Park Dam station are a high of about 26.5 degrees and a low of about 5.9 degrees F, so the warm water is a nice end to the day. See our snowmobiling guide and winter in Island Park.

Summer evenings under the stars

Summer days are warm and the nights are cool. NOAA's July normals for the same station are a high of about 77 degrees and a low of about 43 degrees F. After a day fishing the Henrys Fork, driving up Sawtell Peak or visiting Yellowstone, a soak at dusk is easy. See the best time to visit.
